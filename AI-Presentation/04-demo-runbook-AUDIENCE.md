# Live Demo Runbook — Audience Handout
## "Using AI in Our Lives" — try these yourself, all free

> This is your follow-along guide to the four live demos. Every tool is free, and every prompt below is copy-paste ready. You don't need to keep up during the session — take this home and run each demo at your own pace. **Total: ~8 minutes of demos.**

**Before you start (optional, to follow along):** have a Google account handy (for NotebookLM, Gmail, Calendar) and sign up free at [gamma.app](https://gamma.app) and [canva.com](https://www.canva.com).

---

## 🎬 Demo 1 — Turn messy notes into a stunning slide deck (~2 min)
**Tool:** [Gamma](https://gamma.app) (free) · Alternatives: Canva Magic Design, ChatSlide

**Steps**
1. Open Gamma → **Create new** → **Paste in text**.
2. Paste in any notes you have (lecture notes, meeting notes, a rough outline).
3. Set it to **8 cards**, tone **"Professional & friendly"**, and paste the prompt below.
4. Click **Generate** — a designed deck appears in about 30 seconds.
5. Change the **Theme** at the top to instantly restyle the whole deck.

**Prompt (paste into the instructions box):**
```
Turn the notes I pasted into a clean, engaging 8-slide presentation for a general
adult audience. Requirements:
- Slide 1: a punchy title slide with a one-line hook.
- One clear idea per slide, max 6 words per bullet, max 4 bullets per slide.
- Rewrite my rough notes into confident, plain-English headlines.
- Add one relevant image or icon per slide.
- End with a "3 things to try tonight" takeaway slide.
- Keep all facts strictly to what's in my notes; do not invent statistics.
```

**Remember:** re-check any facts and match your own colours before presenting.

## 🎬 Demo 2 — Turn your study notes into a quiz + a podcast (~2 min)
**Tool:** [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google) (free with any Google account)

**Steps**
1. Open NotebookLM → **Create notebook** → upload a reading (PDF, doc, or even a YouTube link).
2. In the **Studio** panel, click **Study guide**, **Quiz**, or **Flashcards** — generated in seconds.
3. Ask questions in the chat; every answer cites the exact line in your source.
4. In **Studio**, click **Audio Overview** to create a two-host podcast of your notes. *(This takes a few minutes to generate.)*

**Prompt (paste into the chat):**
```
From my source, create a 5-question self-test that gets progressively harder,
then hide the answers below a line so I can check myself afterwards. Focus on the
concepts I'm most likely to be examined on, and cite the section each question
comes from.
```

**Study-as-a-tutor prompt (bonus):**
```
Act as my tutor for this material. Explain "encoding vs. retrieval" using a simple
everyday analogy, then ask me two questions to check I understood. Do not give me
the answers until I respond.
```

**Remember:** NotebookLM only uses what you upload — feed it good sources.

## 🎬 Demo 3 — Draft an email → Calendar event → Meet link (~2 min)
**Tools:** [Gemini app](https://gemini.google.com) + Gmail + Google Calendar + Google Meet (all free)

**Steps**
1. In the free **Gemini app**, paste **Prompt A** to draft a meeting invitation email.
2. Paste that draft into a new email in **Gmail**. Because it names a date/time, Gmail shows **"Add to Calendar"** — click it and the event auto-fills.
3. Open the event in **Google Calendar** → **Add Google Meet video conferencing** → **Save**. The Meet link goes out with the invite.
4. Back in Gemini, paste **Prompt B** to get an agenda and talking points.

**Prompt A — draft the invite:**
```
Write a short, friendly meeting invitation email.
Purpose: kick-off for our "AI in Our Lives" learning session.
Attendees: my team (5 people).
Proposed time: next Tuesday 3:00–3:45 PM.
Include: a one-line purpose, a 3-bullet agenda, and a line saying a Google Meet
link is attached. Keep it under 120 words, warm but professional.
```

**Prompt B — meeting prep:**
```
Create a 45-minute agenda for the session above with rough timings, 3 discussion
questions to open the room, and a list of what I should prepare beforehand.
```

**Good to know:** the deepest features (auto-finding times across colleagues' calendars, automatic Meet notes) need a paid Google Workspace plan. The steps above all work on a **free** account.

## 🎬 Demo 4 — Create a poster / EDM from a description (~2 min)
**Tools:** [Canva](https://www.canva.com) (editable) · [Ideogram](https://ideogram.ai) (text-in-image) · Microsoft Designer (free alt)

**Steps**
1. Open Canva → search **"Poster"** → **Design with AI / Magic Design**.
2. Paste the prompt below.
3. Pick a layout — it opens fully editable. Change the date, colours, or text in seconds.
4. **Download** as PNG/PDF. Use Canva **Resize** to make an email-header version for an EDM.

**Prompt (Canva):**
```
Create a modern, eye-catching event poster (portrait) for a staff learning session.
Title: "Using AI in Our Lives"
Subtitle: "A hands-on 45-minute session — free tools only"
Details to include: Date [Fri 22 Aug], Time [3:00 PM], Venue [Training Room 2],
"Bring your laptop."
Style: clean, friendly, tech-forward; blue and teal palette; lots of white space;
one simple AI/lightbulb motif. Leave text fully editable.
```

**Prompt (Ideogram — if you want the headline baked into the artwork):**
```
A vibrant, modern event poster with the bold headline text "USING AI IN OUR LIVES"
clearly readable at the top, subtitle "Free tools, real results — 45-min session"
below. Flat vector illustration style, blue and teal, a glowing lightbulb made of
circuit lines, plenty of clean space. High legibility text.
```

**Remember:** proofread every word in AI-generated images, and note free tiers usually can't be used commercially and may add a watermark.

| Job | Tool |
|---|---|
| Everyday questions, drafting, travel | **ChatGPT** or **Google Gemini** |
