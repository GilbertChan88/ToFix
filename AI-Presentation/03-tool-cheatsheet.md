# Free AI Tools Cheat-Sheet (2026) — Audience Handout
### One page per topic. Print this or share as a PDF/EDM after the session.
> Unbiased by design: each row lists what a tool is **great at** and its **catch**. "Free tier" means usable without paying, though limits/watermarks may apply. Tool details change fast — verify before relying on specifics.

---

## Quick pick — if you only install four
| Job | Reach for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday questions, drafting, travel | **ChatGPT** or **Google Gemini** | Do 90% of daily tasks |
| Studying & understanding documents | **NotebookLM** | Grounded in *your* files, cites sources |
| Anything visual (poster, EDM, simple video) | **Canva** | Fast, editable, generous free tier |
| Presentations | **Gamma** | Prompt → designed deck in seconds |

## 2.1 Presentation preparation
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Gamma** | Prompt/notes → full designed deck in ~30s | Free credits limited; can look generic; verify facts |
| **Canva Magic Design** | Best *fully free* option, huge template library | More manual styling than Gamma |
| **ChatSlide** | Accepts PDF / URL / YouTube as input; PDF export free | Smaller ecosystem |
| **Copilot (PowerPoint) / Gemini (Slides)** | Builds slides *inside* Office/Google | Best features often need paid plans |

## 2.2 Study
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **NotebookLM** | Summaries, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, **Audio Overview podcast** — all from your sources, with citations | Only knows what you upload; ~50 chats/day free |
| **ChatGPT / Gemini (study mode)** | Explaining concepts, Socratic tutoring | Can hallucinate; not grounded in your notes |
| **Quizlet / Khan-style tutors** | Structured practice sets | Narrower scope |

## 2.3 Email · scheduling · meetings
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Gmail "Help me write"** | Drafting/refining replies | Tone can be generic — edit it |
| **Gmail → "Add to Calendar"** | One-tap event from email text | Detection isn't 100% |
| **Google Meet AI notes / recap** | Capturing notes so you can focus | Full transcription = paid Workspace |
| **Gemini app** | The free "glue": draft, summarise, prep agendas | Doesn't auto-coordinate calendars on free |

## 2.4 Assignment guidance (learn, don't cheat)
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **NotebookLM Learning Guide** | Tutor mode grounded in your material | Needs good source docs |
| **ChatGPT / Gemini (as Socratic tutor)** | Explaining the question, quizzing you | Will write the whole thing if you let it — use the tutor prompt |
> **Steal this prompt:** *"Act as my Socratic tutor. Here is my assignment brief: [paste]. Explain what the question is really asking and the key concepts I need. Then ask me questions to check my understanding. Do NOT write the answer for me."* Always check your institution's AI policy.

## 2.5 EDM / poster creation
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva Magic Design** | Fast, fully editable, resize poster→EDM in a click | Free media/features limited |
| **Microsoft Designer** | Genuinely free social graphics | Fewer templates than Canva |
| **Ideogram** | Best at readable **text inside** AI images | Text baked in — proofread; editing limited |
| **Adobe Express (Firefly)** | Commercial-safe imagery | Small free credit pool |

## 2.6 Video creation
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva / CapCut** | Script/slides → narrated video; watermark-free path | Template-driven, not "generated" cinema |
| **Google Veo (via Gemini)** | Most realistic text→video, with sound | Short clips, small daily free allowance |
| **Runway / Kling / Luma** | Creative control, styles | Watermarks, one-time credits, **no commercial use free** |

## 2.7 Holiday planning
| Tool | Great at | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / Gemini** | Day-by-day itineraries from a vague idea | **Verify prices, hours, that places exist** |
| **Wonderplan** | Free budget breakdowns | No booking integration |
| **Google Travel** | Maps, real listings | Less conversational |
| **Mindtrip / Layla** | All-in-one planner + maps | Best features may be gated |

## 2.8 Daily life — quick wins
- Meals from a fridge photo · explain a confusing letter/contract · summarise a long PDF · soften an awkward message · translate a menu with your camera · draft the toast/poem you're dreading.
- **Never paste** passwords, ID numbers, medical or financial details into free consumer AI.

---

## The 5 habits (repeat to audience)
1. Give context + an example of "good."  2. Ask for its reasoning/sources.  3. Verify facts elsewhere.  4. Iterate — don't accept draft 1.  5. Keep the final decision human.

## The 4 limitations (repeat to audience)
**Hallucinations** (confident + wrong) · **Stale info** (check dates/prices) · **Bias** (mirrors training data) · **Privacy** (free ≠ private).
