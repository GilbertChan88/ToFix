# 📁 START HERE — "Using AI in Our Lives" Session Kit

A complete, ready-to-run kit for your **45-minute sharing session** on using AI in daily life.
Built on the assumption that **your audience only has free AI tools**. Unbiased throughout — every tool comes with its pros *and* its catch.

## The 45-minute flow at a glance
1. **Opening** (3 min) — ground rules, the "AI = fast intern" mental model
2. **Evolution of AI** (4 min) — 1950s → generative AI for everyone
3. **8 everyday jobs** with the best *free* tool for each:
   - 2.1 Presentations → **🎬 Demo 1 (Gamma)**
   - 2.2 Study → **🎬 Demo 2 (NotebookLM podcast)**
   - 2.3 Email/Calendar/Meet → **🎬 Demo 3**
   - 2.4 Assignment guidance (tutor-not-author prompt)
   - 2.5 EDM/Poster → **🎬 Demo 4 (Canva/Ideogram)**
   - 2.6 Video · 2.7 Holiday planning · 2.8 Daily life
4. **Doing it responsibly** (3 min) — limitations + 5 good habits
5. **Close + Q&A** (~12 min buffer)

Full timing table is at the bottom of `01-presentation-with-speaker-notes.md`.

## Your prep checklist
- [ ] Build slides: paste `01-...` content into **Gamma** or **Canva**, or rebuild manually.
- [ ] Log in ahead of time: Gamma, NotebookLM (Google), Gmail/Calendar, Canva.
- [ ] **Pre-generate Demo 2's Audio Overview** (it's slow — never do it live).
- [ ] Record a 45-sec screen capture of Demo 3 as a fallback (email demos are fragile).
- [ ] Optionally pre-make one Canva + one Ideogram poster for Demo 4 comparison.
- [ ] Have fallback screenshots open in browser tabs for each demo.
- [ ] Zoom browser to 125–150% for readability.

## The 4 "wow" moments (this is your engagement engine)
1. **Gamma** — a wall of messy notes → designed deck in ~30 seconds.
2. **NotebookLM** — your own study notes played back as a two-host **podcast**.
3. **Gmail→Calendar** — jumping from an email's text to a real event in one tap.
4. **Canva** — live-editing the date on a finished poster ("try that with a flat JPG").

---

## A note on accuracy
Tool names, free-tier limits, and features in this kit reflect **2026** and change quickly (e.g., NotebookLM is being renamed "Gemini Notebook"; Veo's free allowance shifts). Re-check specifics the week of your talk, and keep the framing honest: **free ≠ private, and AI can be confidently wrong.**
