### Use these details so the poster is about the very session you're running — a nice meta touch.
> Edit the bracketed details to your real date/venue before the session. Copy the prompt for your chosen tool.

---

## The brief
- **Event:** "Using AI in Our Lives" — a hands-on staff learning session
- **Hook:** Free tools, real results
- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Audience:** colleagues/students, mixed AI experience, free tools only
- **Date/Time:** [Fri 22 Aug, 3:00 PM]  ← EDIT ME
- **Venue:** [Training Room 2 / Google Meet link]  ← EDIT ME
- **Call to action:** "Bring your laptop"
- **Vibe:** clean, friendly, tech-forward; blue & teal; lots of white space; a simple lightbulb / circuit motif
- **Formats needed:** portrait poster (print/notice board) + email header version (EDM)

---

## Prompt A — Canva Magic Design (recommended; stays editable)
```
Create a modern, eye-catching event poster (portrait) for a staff learning session.
Title: "Using AI in Our Lives"
Subtitle: "A hands-on 45-minute session — free tools only"
Details to include: Date [Fri 22 Aug], Time [3:00 PM], Venue [Training Room 2],
"Bring your laptop."
Style: clean, friendly, tech-forward; blue and teal palette; lots of white space;
one simple AI/lightbulb motif. Leave text fully editable.
```
**After generating:** pick a layout → change the date live to prove it's editable → Download PNG/PDF → use Canva **Resize** to make an "Email header" for the EDM version.

## Prompt B — Ideogram (best when you want the headline text baked into art)
```
A vibrant, modern event poster with the bold headline text "USING AI IN OUR LIVES"
clearly readable at the top, subtitle "Free tools, real results — 45-min session"
below. Flat vector illustration style, blue and teal, a glowing lightbulb made of
circuit lines, plenty of clean space. High legibility text.
```

## Prompt C — Microsoft Designer (fully free alternative)
```
Design a clean event poster titled "Using AI in Our Lives", subtitle "Free tools,
real results — a hands-on 45-minute session". Include date Fri 22 Aug, 3:00 PM,
Training Room 2, and "Bring your laptop". Blue-and-teal tech theme, lightbulb icon,
lots of white space, modern sans-serif.
```

## EDM (marketing email) bonus prompt — draft the email body in ChatGPT/Gemini
```
Write a short, upbeat internal invitation email (an EDM) promoting the session on
the poster. 90 words max. Include a one-line hook, three quick "you'll learn to…"
bullets, the date/time/venue, and a clear "Reserve your spot" call to action.
```

## Say this caveat aloud
"Proofread every letter — AI still misspells text inside images — and note free tiers usually forbid commercial use and may add a watermark."
